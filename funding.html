Funding control checklist

Offers must pass all gates before publication.

  • Unique opportunity reference and approved capacity
  • 120-day standard tenure; optional periods require approvals
  • Legal and finance approval by different authorised users
  • Management and tax references for non-standard terms
  • Verified KYC and signed hard-copy agreement
  • Only verified receipts activate funding

Segregated investor records

Short-term funders are maintained separately from the ordinary equity register. Existing shareholders may be linked to a funder profile, but funding does not change shareholding.

Approval & publication rules

  1. Create draft and verify all terms.
  2. Submit for approval.
  3. Legal approver records legal clearance and reference.
  4. A different finance approver records finance and terms approval.
  5. Optional tenure and tax settings require management/tax references.
  6. Only then can an authorised manager open the offer.
No default or illustrative return rate is pre-approved. The opportunity cannot be opened without a recorded rate and approval evidence.
